# Uno

Uno is a shedding-type card game for 2-10 players in which players race to empty their hands by matching the top discard by colour, number or symbol.

## 1. Components

- **Deck (standard, 108 cards)**: four colours (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue), each with 25 cards:
  - one `0`
  - two each of `1`-`9`
  - two each of `Skip`, `Reverse`, `Draw Two`
  - plus 4 `Wild` and 4 `Wild Draw Four` (colourless).
- **Players**: 2-10 (publisher recommends age 7+). Play direction starts clockwise.
- Zones: face-down **draw pile**, face-up **discard pile**, one hand per player.
- Newer retail sets (about 112 cards) add extra Wild-type cards (e.g. Wild Swap Hands, Wild Shuffle Hands, customisable wilds). These are not part of the main body (see Appendix A).

## 2. Objective

Be the first to play all cards in your hand. In a multi-round game, score points from opponents' remaining cards and be first to reach 500.

## 3. Setup and dealing

1. Choose the dealer: each player draws a card, and the highest number deals. This step is optional in an engine and can be replaced by a fixed or random choice.
2. The dealer shuffles and deals **7 cards** to each player, face down.
3. Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile. Turn the top card face up to start the discard pile.
4. **Starting card handling:**
   - If it is a **Wild Draw Four**: return it to the draw pile, reshuffle and flip a new card.
   - If it is a **Wild**: the player to the dealer's left chooses the starting colour, then plays first.
   - If it is a **Skip**: the player to the dealer's left is skipped, so the next player after them goes first.
   - If it is a **Reverse**: the dealer plays first and direction is counterclockwise.
   - If it is a **Draw Two**: the player to the dealer's left draws 2 and loses their turn.
5. Otherwise the player to the dealer's left plays first (clockwise).

## 4. Turn structure

On your turn, do exactly one of the following.

1. **Play a card** from your hand that matches the top discard by **colour**, by **number**, or by **symbol/action** (e.g. Skip on Skip). A Wild may be played on anything. A Wild Draw Four may be played only under the condition in Section 5.
   - Only **one card** per turn. Stacking several cards, or stacking penalty cards, is not allowed.
2. **Draw** if you have no playable card (or choose not to play, see Section 8): draw **one** card from the draw pile.
   - If the drawn card is playable, you may play it immediately.
   - Otherwise keep it and your turn ends.
   - You may not play any card that was already in your hand on that turn after drawing.
3. Resolve the played card's effect (Section 5), then play passes to the next player in the current direction.
4. **Saying "Uno"**: when you play your second-to-last card (leaving one card in hand) you must call "Uno" before the next player's turn begins. If another player catches you not having called it before the next player takes their turn, you draw **2** cards as a penalty. This applies each time you drop to one card.
5. **Ending the round**: when a player plays their last card, the round ends immediately (an action effect on the last card still applies when scoring is by hand, e.g. the next player in a multi-player game draws for Draw Two/Draw Four, and those cards count toward that player's hand when scored under official scoring).

## 5. Special cards

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| Number (0-9) | No effect. |
| **Skip** | Next player in turn order loses their turn. |
| **Reverse** | Direction of play reverses. In a 2-player game it acts as Skip (you play again). |
| **Draw Two** | Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn. |
| **Wild** | Playable on any card. The player names the colour that must be matched next (any colour, including the current one). |
| **Wild Draw Four** | Player names the next colour. Next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn, unless they successfully challenge. Legal to play **only if you have no card matching the colour of the current top discard** (you may still hold number or symbol matches or Wilds, per official rules). |

**Wild Draw Four challenge**: the player who would draw may challenge.
- The player who played it shows their hand privately to the challenger.
- If it was **illegal** (they held a card of the required colour), the player who played it draws **4** cards instead, and the challenger does not draw and takes their turn normally.
- If it was **legal**, the challenger draws **6** cards (4 + 2 penalty) and loses their turn.

## 6. Terms

- **Top discard / active colour**: the colour to match; after a Wild it is the declared colour.
- **Penultimate card**: the second-to-last card, played when calling "Uno".
- **Draw pile / discard pile**: stock and waste.

## 7. Scoring and winning

- **Single round (current official rules)**: the first player to empty their hand wins.
- **Cumulative scoring (traditional, to 500)**: the winner scores the total of all cards left in opponents' hands:
  - Number cards: face value.
  - Skip, Reverse, Draw Two: 20 each.
  - Wild, Wild Draw Four: 50 each.
  - Newer special wilds (Swap Hands, customisable): 40 each.
- The first player to reach **500 points** wins the game. Otherwise start a new round (new shuffle and deal; the round's winner or the next dealer rotates).

## 8. Edge cases and clarifications

- **Draw pile exhausted**: set aside the top discard, shuffle the rest of the discard pile to form a new draw pile, then turn the set-aside card face up again.
- **Drawn card voluntary**: after drawing, play is optional; many sources also allow a player to draw even when holding a playable card, though not every source states this explicitly (see Appendix A).
- **Draw Two and Draw Four penalties cannot be answered** (no stacking or deflecting) in the main rules; the victim simply draws and loses their turn.
- **Two-player game**: Reverse acts as Skip. Skip lets the player play again. After a Draw Two or Wild Draw Four the opponent draws and the player plays again.
- **Last card is a special Wild** (Swap Hands, Shuffle Hands): treat as a plain Wild with no extra effect.
- **Wild chosen colour** may be any of the four colours, including the colour already active.
- **Forgetting Uno** is penalised only if caught before the next player's turn begins; once the next player has played or drawn, it is too late.
- **Reverse and Skip as first card** are resolved as in Section 3.

## Appendix A: Rule Variations

Items marked **(differs)** contradict the main body; the others are additions.

- **Stacking draw cards (differs)**: a player facing a Draw Two may play another Draw Two (and likewise Draw Four on Draw Four) to pass the penalty, so the next player draws the accumulated total. Widespread house rule, but explicitly not official.
- **Seven-O (addition)**: playing a 7 lets you swap hands with any player; playing a 0 makes all players pass their hands in the current direction.
- **Jump-In (addition)**: a player holding an identical card (same colour and number) may play it out of turn; play continues from that player.
- **Wild Draw Four legality (differs)**: some versions say it may be played only if you hold no card of the colour **or** matching number/symbol (stricter), or allow it at any time (no challenge).
- **Challenge rule (differs)**: older official rules had a challenge penalty of 4 for the loser of the challenge and a different order of effects; the 6-card penalty for a failed challenge is the current official version.
- **Drawing until playable (differs)**: some players draw until they can play a card, rather than a single draw.
- **Forced play (differs)**: some versions require playing a playable drawn card.
- **Penalty on Uno miss**: some groups use 4 cards instead of 2.
- **Winning on an action card**: some groups do not allow finishing with an action or Wild card.
- **Scoring (differs)**: some play a single round only (the current official rules); others play to 500, or play to a different target, or low-score-wins variants where players accumulate card points and the game ends at a threshold.
- **Draw Four on first card**: some groups simply play it with the first player drawing 4 instead of reshuffling.
- **Newer card sets** (Wild Swap Hands, Wild Shuffle Hands, Wild Customizable, Wild Draw Two, etc.) add effects not in the base deck and score 40 in cumulative games.
