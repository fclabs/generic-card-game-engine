# Go Fish

A simple rank-matching card game for 2-6 players in which players ask each other for cards to collect "books" of four cards of the same rank; the player with the most books wins.

## 1. Components

- One standard 52-card deck (no jokers in the base game). Suit is irrelevant; only rank matters (A, 2-10, J, Q, K).
- Players: 2 to 5 is the usual range (up to 6 or more is common in informal play; the base rules hold with a smaller hand size).
- Zones: each player's hand (hidden), a shared face-down **stock** (draw pile), and each player's face-up **books** area.

## 2. Objective

Collect the most books. A **book** is all four cards of one rank. There are 13 books in total.

## 3. Setup and Dealing

1. Choose a dealer (any method). Shuffle the deck.
2. Deal clockwise, one card at a time:
   - 2 or 3 players: **7 cards** each.
   - 4 or more players: **5 cards** each.
3. Place the remaining cards face down as the stock.
4. The player to the dealer's left takes the first turn. Play proceeds clockwise.
5. If any player is dealt a complete book (four of a rank), they lay it face up at once (see Edge Cases).

## 4. Turn Structure

On your turn:

1. **Ask.** Choose one opponent and name a rank. You must hold at least one card of that rank in your hand.
2. **Respond.**
   - If the asked player holds any cards of that rank, they must hand over **all** of them to you. You add them to your hand. You are **successful** and your turn continues (go to step 4).
   - If they hold none, they say "Go fish!"
3. **Fish.** On "Go fish!" you draw the top card of the stock and add it to your hand. Your turn then ends and play passes to the left. (If the stock is empty, you draw nothing and the turn simply passes.)
4. **Check for books.** If you now hold four cards of one rank, lay them face up in front of you as a book. Completing a book does not by itself end or extend your turn beyond the rules above (but see below: a successful ask already grants another turn).
5. **Continue.** After a successful ask, return to step 1 (ask again, same or different opponent or rank). Your turn ends only when you are told "Go fish!" (or, if you have no cards and the stock is empty, you cannot act).

Information rules: the ask and the outcome (cards transferred or "Go fish") are public. The number of cards transferred is known to all. Players must answer truthfully.

## 5. Special Cards, Actions, and Terms

- **Book:** four cards of the same rank, laid face up; it is out of play permanently.
- **Ask / request:** naming a rank to a specific opponent.
- **Go fish:** the response when the asked player has no card of the named rank.
- **Stock / pond / ocean:** the face-down draw pile.
- There are no special cards in the base game; all ranks behave identically.

## 6. Scoring and End of Game

- Each book scores 1 point.
- The game ends when all 13 books have been laid down.
- The player with the most books wins. Ties are possible (a tie is a shared win unless players agree to a tiebreak or replay).

## 7. Edge Cases and Clarifications

- **Empty hand with stock remaining:** at the start of your turn, if your hand is empty, draw one card from the stock and then take your turn normally (the majority convention). If the stock is also empty, you are out of the game and take no further turns; remaining players continue.
- **Empty hand during a turn:** if a player has just laid down their last cards as a book mid-turn, they draw from the stock if available; if the stock is empty they are out.
- **Empty stock:** play continues without fishing. A player whose ask fails simply has their turn pass. Play goes on until all 13 books are made (or until no player holds cards).
- **Asking requirement:** you may never ask for a rank you do not hold.
- **Asking a player with no cards:** allowed; the answer is "Go fish."
- **Books dealt initially:** laid down immediately and count as books.
- **Books formed by fishing:** a drawn card that completes a book is laid down at once and counts. (Base rule: the turn still passes, since the ask failed; see Appendix for the lucky-catch variant.)
- **Hand reveal:** hands are never shown except when books are laid down or cards are handed over.
- **Multiple cards:** the asked player must give every card of the rank, not just one.

## Appendix A: Rule Variations

Differences from the main body are marked.

1. **Lucky catch / fish-wish (differs: turn continuation).** If the card drawn after "Go fish" is the rank you asked for (some sources: or completes a book), you show it and take another turn. Wikipedia and officialgamerules.org describe this as common; Bicycle's stricter version passes the turn after any failed ask.
2. **Completing a book grants another turn (differs).** Some sources give an extra turn whenever you complete a book, including by fishing.
3. **Deal counts (differs).** Some sources use 7 cards for 2-3 players and 5 for 4-5 (same as body); others use 5 for 3 or more or 7 for any number of players.
4. **Game end on first empty hand (differs).** Alternative: the game ends when any player empties their hand by laying down books, either declaring that player the winner or awarding the win to whoever has the most books at that moment. The main body uses "all 13 books made".
5. **Empty-hand rule (differs).** Some rules: a player with no cards and an empty stock is simply out (same as body). Others: on an empty hand, the player draws up to a full hand (5 or 7) if possible, or the turn passes without a draw.
6. **Pairs (differs: children's version).** Collect two matching cards (pairs) instead of four-card books; most pairs wins.
7. **Exact-count asking (differs).** The asker must state how many cards of the rank they want ("Do you have two 3s?").
8. **Give one card only (differs).** The asked player gives just one matching card rather than all.
9. **Asking for specific cards (differs).** Ask for a particular rank and suit.
10. **Jokers wild (differs).** Jokers added as wild cards.
11. **Memory rounds / "no sneaky cards" (differs).** Players must remember prior asks; truthful answers are emphasized in some house rules.
